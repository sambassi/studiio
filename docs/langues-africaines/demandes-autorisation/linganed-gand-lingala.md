**To:** Linganed team, Ghent University (contact address from the Linganed legal notice / website — to be confirmed)
**Subject:** Request for permission to reuse Lingala dictionary data in a commercial software interface

Dear Linganed team,

I run Studiio (studiio.pro), a **commercial** subscription platform for creating social-media videos. We are translating our interface into several African languages, including Lingala, so that our users can use the product in their language.

We have read the legal notice of the Linganed online dictionary, which prohibits copying, including repeated or automated queries. We therefore **have not used Linganed** and have not copied any of its content.

**We respectfully request permission to:**
1. use selected Linganed entries (Lingala words and their French/English/Dutch equivalents) as a source for **translations integrated into the user interface of our commercial application**;
2. if possible, receive an export or a licence for a limited list of terms (a few hundred interface words: dashboard, video creation, calendar, credits, payments, subscriptions…);
3. know the required attribution (we would credit the Linganed project, the authors and Ghent University — Michael Meeuwis — on a credits page and in our admin tool);
4. optionally, be put in touch with a Lingala speaker or linguist able to review our translations.

**Our commitments:** no redistribution of the dictionary; only the terms needed for the interface; every translation reviewed by a native speaker before publication and labelled "to be validated" until then; strict compliance with any condition you set. Until we hear from you we use no Linganed data.
